Today’s catch: Bakmie Jojon.

Today’s catch is Bakmie Jojon, and the taste is seriously mantap. The best part is you get to pick your own noodles, from the chewy bouncy kind to the flat wide ones that feel like kwetiau. Every bite hits different depending on your pick, so it never gets boring. I rate every spot on taste, vibe, and price, and this bowl is going straight on my must-visit list. Weekend hunt: sweet tooth edition.

This weekend’s hunt was Scoup ice cream in Bandung, and I brought the squad. Good food always tastes better with good friends, and this one was a total win. We all grabbed a scoop, took way too many photos, and forgot about everything else for a while. Dessert is a personal responsibility, and this crew took it seriously.
